## Root cause (confirmed vs likely)

- *Confirmed:* the failure is a **D2D vertical-orientation local-memory bandwidth shortfall on the
  two inner die-pairs (die1, die2): ~275–284 GB/s vs the 360 GB/s fail-threshold**, while the edge
  die-pairs (die0, die3) pass at ~400. Zero data/HW/link errors; ran to completion; no hang.
- *Confirmed:* 11910's D2D assertion was never fixed — its close was EDP-only (PR #342) + a
  test-reorder. So the die_2_die_bw D2D failure has been latent the whole time.
- *Unverified (and the reason NOT to call this a straight dup of 11910):* 11910's ticket records no
  per-op breakdown, so I cannot prove its D2D half was this same inner-die vertical-lmem deficit vs
  some other die_2_die_bw metric. Same test + station + image, but the sub-signature match is
  unproven.
- *Open question for the owner:* is the ~30% inner-die vertical-lmem deficit (a) a genuine D2D/NoC
  bandwidth limitation for inner die-pairs on image `2_18_0_2p0_1240`, or (b) a qual fail-threshold
  (uniform 360) that doesn't account for the inner-die vertical topology on Palladium? The clean,
  reproducible edge≈400 / inner≈280 split points at a structural cause, not flakiness.
